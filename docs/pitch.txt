One-line pitch: Nest makes flexible residential rentals simple, helping people find, compare, and book verified homes, rooms, and condos in Cambodia from days to months.

Problem & who has it:
Finding a reliable place to stay in Cambodia for a few days, weeks, or months is fragmented. Renters rely on Facebook, classifieds, agents, and hotel platforms, making it difficult to compare properties, verify listings, check availability, and book with confidence. Property owners also struggle to monetize vacant rooms and units. Our target users are travelers, students, professionals, families, and property owners.

Your solution / what you'll build:
Nest is a Cambodia-first marketplace for flexible residential stays. Renters can discover verified condos, rooms, apartments, service apartments, and homes, compare prices and amenities, check availability, and book for the period they need. Property owners can list their properties, manage availability, receive bookings, and track their income through one platform.

Why now? What changed that makes this possible?
More people are looking for flexible living options, while Cambodia has a growing supply of condos, apartments, rooms, and residential properties. However, rental discovery and booking remain fragmented across social media, classified platforms, agents, and accommodation platforms. At the same time, AI and vibe-coding significantly reduce the cost and time required to build and test a marketplace, allowing us to validate the idea quickly with real users.

Progress so far:
We are currently at the early validation stage. We have defined the core problem, target users, MVP scope, business model, and initial competitive landscape. Our next step is to build the MVP and validate it with renters and property owners in Phnom Penh.

Competitors/alternatives - what do you understand that they don’t?
Our alternatives include Khmer24, Realestate.com.kh, Airbnb, Facebook rental groups, and traditional real-estate agents. We believe the gap is between property discovery and actual rental booking. Classifieds and social media provide listings but often require manual communication and negotiation, while hotel platforms focus mainly on short stays. Nest focuses specifically on flexible residential rentals, combining verified properties, availability, transparent pricing, booking, payment, and renter-owner communication in one experience.

How will this make money?
Nest charges a one-time placement fee per completed booking, deducted from the first month’s rent. There is no recurring commission.
The fee scales with term: 50% of one month’s rent for stays of one to three months, 75% for four to six months, and 100% for seven to twelve months. Renters pay nothing. This sits below the customary agent commission of one full month’s rent, and applies to the shorter durations agents rarely service.

What are your goals for the program?
Our primary goal is to turn Nest from an idea into a validated MVP. We want to launch the core renter and owner journeys, onboard our first properties, test real rental demand, and collect feedback from both sides of the marketplace. We also want to validate our pricing and commission model, identify our strongest initial market segment, and finish the program with evidence that we are solving a real problem rather than simply building another property listing platform.
